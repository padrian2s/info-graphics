III

The Balance Point & the 20ms Train

Lines 75–145

🚂 Diagram: The Train Departs Every 20ms

A new batch starts every ~20ms regardless of fill level. Worst-case queue latency = 20ms wait + 20ms run = 40ms.
Each train = batch ≈ 300 × sparsity tokens
Worst-case queue arrival
Why 20ms? It's HBM_capacity / HBM_bandwidth — the time to read all of HBM once. Reading it twice would be wasteful (weights and KVs are read-only).

Lines 415–495

⚖ Diagram: Equalize Pre-training, RL & Inference Cost

Heuristic: minimum total cost ≈ where the three are equal. Solving with α≈1/10 (RL inefficiency) gives D_pre ≈ D_RL ≈ D_inf. Today's models are ~100× over-trained vs. Chinchilla.
